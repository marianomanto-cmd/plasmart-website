Frequently
asked questions

[ FAQ ]
What services does Plasmart offer?

We do laser cutting (up to 12.7 mm), high-definition plasma cutting (up to 32 mm), CNC bending (up to 3 m) and general metalwork. On top of that, our own design and advisory team takes your project from idea to finished part.

Are designs limited to the catalog?

Not at all. The catalog is just a starting point: we have access to thousands of ready-to-cut patterns and designs, and we also custom-make whatever idea you bring. Send us a sketch, a photo or a reference and our design team turns it into a one-of-a-kind steel piece. Your project leads the way — design has no limit.

Where is Plasmart located and how long has it been operating?

We're in Córdoba (Francisco de Arteaga 2895), with our own plant and over 20 years cutting steel, since 2006. That track record is what lets us commit to deadlines and deliver parts right the first time.

What thickness can you cut?

Up to 32 mm with high-definition plasma and up to 12.7 mm with laser. We recommend the right process for your part: laser when precision and detail matter, plasma when thickness is the priority.

Do you ship across the country?

Yes, we ship throughout Argentina. We arrange the shipment with the carrier you prefer or the one that best serves your area; freight is quoted separately depending on the destination. Typical delivery in 5 to 7 days.

How do I request a quote?

Message us on WhatsApp at +54 9 351 382 0321 with your file (DXF or STEP) or just tell us the idea. We send a detailed quote within 24 to 48 hours, with committed lead times.

Do you work for architecture and industry?

Yes, both. In architecture we make façades, decorative panels and stairs; in industry, metalworking, agricultural parts and batch production.
